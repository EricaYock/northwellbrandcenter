Logo Hierarchy

Across our health system, the Northwell name and constellation symbol anchor our visual identity. For a specific subset of entities and service lines, we use a locked-up name within the logo. For all other programs, practices, offices, units, etc., we use a text-based descriptor treatment in support of our system brand.

Note: Physician Partners does not exist in the Connecticut & Hudson Valley regions.

Nomenclature

If you did not see your practice or program in our downloadable files, it falls within our descriptive treatment within the logo hierarchy. A logo lock-up will not be created; however, it is displayed with a dedicated Northwell logo with the text underneath with proper spacing.
