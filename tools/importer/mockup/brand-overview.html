Better health is a journey that’s most successful with a partner by your side, and all New Yorkers should experience the confidence and peace of mind that comes from getting world-class care wherever they are. We’re here to close the gaps and change the status quo—helping with needs from big to small for a healthier you and a healthier world.

Color Palette

Our color palette is bright, vibrant and diverse. Our colors work together to represent the breadth and depth of our expertise while evoking a sense of inspiration and optimism.

Because our color palette is so diverse, we have established a collection of approved color pairings that will guide our approach to color and ensure that our visual brand identity is well maintained and represented.

Logo Formats

We have created five configurations of our logo to offer maximum flexibility across a variety of applications. The vertical and horizontal configurations are preferred and should be used in print and digital communications whenever possible.

Typography

The Galaxie type family of families encompasses the primary typefaces for Northwell. Galaxie comprises three type families that allow flexibility for all communication needs. The families include Galaxie Polaris Condensed, Galaxie Polaris and Galaxie Copernicus.
